THE GAP
Utilities' current answer is a blanket export cap — safe, but it throws away clean generation whether or not a violation would actually occur that day.

 No accessible, forecast-driven tool exists today that decides, feeder by feeder and hour by hour, whether a cap is even necessary — and what a lighter-touch fix would cost.
THE TECHNICAL PROBLEM
On sunny afternoons, hundreds of homes on the same feeder export simultaneously while demand is at its daily low — pushing bus voltage above the safe 1.05 p.u. limit and loading lines beyond thermal rating.    

 This isn't rare or theoretical: it's the direct, mechanical consequence of the adoption curve above, and it happens on ordinary residential feeders, not just at utility scale.

“Why is this a true digital twin instead of just a static dashboard?" 
We have, Forecaster →  Power-Flow Engine →  Corrective Action Engine
Most dashboards only show what happened or happening. Our architecture actively runs predictive physics simulations on future forecasts and automatically tests solutions before problems even occur in the physical world. We implement Agentic AI system to ensure no issues make system haywire and we never miss out on anything at all without halucination.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
Reliable Physics Engine
Uses pandapower (runpp) for deterministic, 
mathematically sound power-flow calculations rather
 than black-box AI guessing
Accessible Data
 Overcomes the lack of proprietary Indian grid data by 
utilizing the peer-reviewed CIGRE MV benchmark network 
and free PVGIS solar data
Efficient Forecasting
 Employs LightGBM (gradient-boosted trees), which trains quickly, is highly explainable, and sets a strong baseline for short-horizon tabular data
Independent Testability
 The pure Python backend (src/) is strictly separated from 
the Streamlit UI (app/), meaning the core logic is
 defensible and can be validated by automated tests 
without UI interference
Rapid Prototyping Constraints
  Intentionally avoids heavy deep learning (like LSTMs) and complex build pipelines, making it rapidly deployable and technically feasible for immediate execution.

Honest Reporting
 Builds utility operator trust by deliberately including 
an infeasible scenario, transparently reporting "unresolved"
 instead of hiding limitations
Prioritizing "Free" Solutions
Maximizes operational viability by attempting non-disruptive, energy-free actions (like feeder reconfiguration) before forcing solar curtailment.
Standardized Tolerances
 Enforces strict real-world safety limits, including a 0.95-1.05 pu voltage band and maximum line loading percentages to prevent thermal overloads
Robust Scenario Testing
 Proven across complex conditions: midday high solar, evening demand peaks, and rapid solar drops due to cloud cover.
Multi-Variable Action Ranking
The engine doesn't just apply the first fix it finds; it systematically scores proposed actions based on resolution success, clean energy retained, and a cost proxy.
